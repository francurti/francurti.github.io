Privacy Policy for Bobail

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Bobail ("the app") is a board game developed by Francisco Curti. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information stored on your device

To keep your progress, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • the skin you have equipped and the skins you have unlocked,
  • your points balance,
  • game statistics: games played and won per difficulty, and time played.

This information never leaves your device. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Your device may include it in its own backups (for example Android's backup to your Google account), which are governed by your device provider's privacy policy.

The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device information, such as model, operating system and language,
  • how you interact with ads.

This is collected by Google, not by the developer. How Google uses it is described in Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from the app's settings. Elsewhere, you can limit personalised ads by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your device settings (on Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads).

The app works offline; ads are only shown when you are connected.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If the app starts handling information differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
